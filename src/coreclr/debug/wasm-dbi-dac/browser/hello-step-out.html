Sets a managed breakpoint at BreakHereInner, then on the first hit issues a step-out request. The step returns to the caller BreakHereOuter. The runtime fires a structured step-complete DBI event; the sidecar DAC drains the WasmDbgIpcEventStepComplete payload via coreclr_wasm_dbi_dac_dbi_poll_ipc_step_complete.
