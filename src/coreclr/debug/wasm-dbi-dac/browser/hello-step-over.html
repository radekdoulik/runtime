Status: running...

Sets a managed breakpoint at BreakHere, then on the first hit issues a step-over request. The step skips the SomeOtherMethod() call and lands at the next statement in BreakHere. The runtime fires a structured step-complete DBI event; the sidecar DAC drains the WasmDbgIpcEventStepComplete payload via coreclr_wasm_dbi_dac_dbi_poll_ipc_step_complete.
